# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Please keep discussions constructive, respectful, and practical.

This repository is meant to help operators and administrators work through real deployment problems with less friction, so clarity and professionalism matter.

## Expected Behavior

- be respectful and direct
- share reproducible details when reporting issues
- assume good intent
- keep technical disagreement focused on the work

## Unacceptable Behavior

- harassment or personal attacks
- intentionally hostile or dismissive communication
- sharing secrets, credentials, or sensitive production details in public issues

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, moderate, or close discussions that do not follow these expectations.
